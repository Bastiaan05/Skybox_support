# Skybox support

Skybox helps you identify the sky, explore the Solar System and plan photographs on iPhone.

For help or to report a problem, email [bw05@xs4all.nl](mailto:bw05@xs4all.nl). Include your iPhone model, iOS version, Skybox version and the steps that led to the problem. Describe what you expected and what happened instead. Do not include private observing locations or saved-plan details unless they are needed and you choose to share them. No diagnostic files are sent automatically.

## Common questions

**Can I use Skybox without camera or location access?** Yes. Choose a site manually and use the virtual sky, Solar System, photography planner and calculators. Camera access is needed only for the camera overlay; location access is optional when choosing your current location.

**What works offline?** Bundled astronomy, stars and selected deep-sky targets, Solar System exploration, manual sites, calculations and saved plans. Apple Maps and place search need an internet connection. Satellite predictions need previously downloaded orbital data that is still within its validity window.

**Why is satellite data unavailable or old?** Satellite elements have a short validity window. Check Settings → Performance & data → Satellite orbital data for element epochs, download and installation status, and the next permitted request. Skybox preserves provider restrictions when clearing its cache or resetting preferences. A reset will not force a download sooner.

**Why does a camera marker differ from what I see?** Phone heading and motion accuracy, calibration, location, buildings and atmospheric conditions can affect alignment or visibility. The virtual view provides a camera-free alternative. Skybox is not a navigation instrument. Do not point optical equipment at the Sun without appropriate solar protection.

**Does this release include a light-pollution map?** No. The first release does not include that layer.

**Where are my plans stored?** Plans, sites and equipment are stored locally. Skybox has no account or app cloud sync. Normal device backups may include saved content. Review the scope shown before using a data-deletion action in Settings.

Read the [Skybox privacy policy](https://github.com/Bastiaan05/Skybox_privacy-policy) for data and privacy details.
